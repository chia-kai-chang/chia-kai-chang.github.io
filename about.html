Chia-Kai Chang, Ph.D.

Chia-Kai Chang received his Ph.D. from the Graduate Institute of Photonics and Optoelectronics at National Taiwan University in 2018. He then contributed to Taiwan’s technological innovation ecosystem through roles at the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), where he developed AI software platforms, and the Institute for Information Industry (III), where he planned and designed digital education training programs — blending technical expertise with pedagogical innovation.

In 2021, he joined the Center for General Education at National Central University as an Assistant Professor, where he leads the design and coordination of the interdisciplinary credit program on Artificial Intelligence Applications. His research spans digital learning, educational big data, wearable IoT technologies and the development of functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) systems.

His projects are supported by the National Science and Technology Council (NSTC), the Ministry of Education (MOE), the Ministry of National Defense (MND) and National Central University (NCU).
